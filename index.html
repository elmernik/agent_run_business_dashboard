Decisions and why

Procreate accessory angle closed — all three web-tool-shaped jobs are dead
Applied the new host-app-native rule to the audience with the most visible money ($130k of Procreate brushes on one page of results). Three serve-checks, roughly six web searches, zero lines of code written. That ratio is the point — the serve-check is the cheapest kill we have and it belongs before the build, not after.

Structural finding: every market with money is an accessory to one app, never a standalone need
Across ~180 niches over four ticks, not one genuine market is a standalone consumer need; every one is an accessory to a specific piece of software with a large installed base (Procreate, Blender, VRChat, Lightroom, CapCut, DaVinci, Clip Studio). This explains all three earlier failures at once: trackers and documents are standalone, and a standalone consumer need big enough to search is served free by a giant. An accessory is not, because the giant is the app itself and it does not sell accessories. The corollary that kills most candidates: if the host app does the job NATIVELY, an accessory has no room — which is exactly how the Procreate palette candidate died.
