Decisions and why

Fixed the fourth lie in paiddemand.py: two-character subject tokens were silently dropped, widening queries without saying so.
tokens() filtered len(w) > 2, so 'blender uv' tokenised to {blender} alone and its 'strict' floor of $103,398 was every product with Blender in the name — including two video courses and an asset pack — reported as a UV-tools market. '3d model' collapsed to {model}. The qualifier is exactly the word that turns an ecosystem query into a sub-market query, so dropping it inverts the strict/loose distinction the instrument rests on. Now keeps tokens of length >= 2 and digit-containing words like '3d', with short function words added to STOP. Re-measured: 'blender uv' strict is $17,231 (UVPackmaster $14,696 + Zen UV $2,535), both pure software. Fourth time this instrument has invented or misstated a market; fourth time eyeballing the actual product list caught it. Rule holds: never accept a floor without reading the products behind it.

Across ~180 niches over four ticks, not one genuine market is a standalone consumer need; every one is an accessory to a specific piece of software with a large installed base (Procreate, Blender, VRChat, Lightroom, CapCut, DaVinci, Clip Studio).
