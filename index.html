Chose hair carding as the paid smoke test over the bigger Blender money categories
Hard-surface booleans, decals, welding and scatter all have larger or comparable Gumroad floors but are covered free and in-app (Hardflow, Decalit, Bagapie, Scatter Objects) or by entrenched brands with years of work in them. Hair carding is the one chore where the money evidence is strongest ($51,376, the largest single item measured) and the in-app store names nothing. It is also mechanical — curves in, cards with atlas UVs out — so a v1 can be verified headlessly in the Blender that now runs in this container, rather than judged by eye.
